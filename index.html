This Privacy Policy explains how Preconcy ("Preconcy", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the Preconcy mobile app and its related services (together, the "Service"). Preconcy helps individuals and couples track menstrual cycles, symptoms and fertility. Because this involves sensitive health information, we keep what we collect to what the app needs to work.

🚫No ads, no sellingWe never sell your data or use it for advertising.
🔒Encrypted in transitAll app traffic uses HTTPS.
💞You control sharingData is shared with a partner only after you both accept.
🗑️Delete anytimeAsk us to delete your account and data.

1Information we collect

We collect only information you provide in the app, plus the minimum technical data needed to run the Service.

👤 Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only as a secure one-way hash)
  • Gender
  • Date of birth
  • Email verification status and one-time codes (OTPs) used for verification and password reset

🌸 Cycle & symptom data Health

  • Period start and end dates
  • Cycle length
  • Predicted ovulation date and fertile window (calculated by us)
  • Daily symptoms: mood, flow, energy level, pain level
  • Any notes you choose to write

💧 Male fertility data Health

  • Dates and times of ejaculation logs you record
  • Method you select for each log
  • Fertility score, day number and phase (calculated by us)

💞 Partner connection

  • Your partner's email address, when you send an invitation
  • Invitation status (pending, accepted, rejected) and related dates

Technical information

  • Authentication token: after you sign in, a login token is stored on your device in encrypted secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) so you stay signed in.
  • Server logs: like most online services, our servers automatically record basic request data such as IP address, date and time, and error details. We use this only for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.
What we don't collect: we don't use advertising IDs, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

2How we use your information

  • Provide the Service: create and manage your account, and store and display your logs and history.
  • Calculate insights: determine your cycle phase, fertile window, ovulation date, chance of pregnancy, fertility score, and show recommendations relevant to your phase.
  • Connect partners: send invitations and, once accepted, show combined fertility insights to both partners.
  • Account security: verify your email, send one-time codes, reset passwords and protect against unauthorized access.
  • Service emails: send essential messages such as verification codes, password resets and partner invitations. We don't send marketing emails.
  • Maintain and improve: fix bugs, keep the Service running reliably, and comply with legal obligations.

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process your account data to perform our contract with you, process health data based on your explicit consent (given when you choose to enter it), and process security logs based on our legitimate interest in keeping the Service safe. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data or your account.

3Partner sharing

Preconcy lets you connect with one partner. Sharing starts only after one person sends an invitation and the other accepts it. Once connected:

  • Your partner can see fertility insights derived from your data, such as cycle phase, fertile window and ovulation date, or fertility score and phase.
  • Your partner can see your name and email address.

You can end the connection at any time from the app. When you do, your partner loses access to your data going forward.

5Storage & security

  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. We can never see your password.
  • Login tokens on your device are kept in the operating system's encrypted secure storage, and are removed when you log out.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel who need it.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will notify you of any breach as required by law.

6Data retention

  • We keep your account and health data for as long as your account is active.
  • One-time codes expire after 10 minutes and are cleared once used.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal and health data within 30 days. Residual copies in backups are overwritten within 90 days.
  • We may keep limited information longer only where the law requires it.

7Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your data and your account
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for processing health data at any time
  • Data portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in Contact us. We respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first.

8Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your Preconcy account and all associated data at any time:

  1. Email support@preconcy.com from the email address registered to your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account".
  3. We'll confirm the request and delete your account, cycle logs, symptom logs, fertility logs and partner connection within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Please send a deletion request if you want your data removed.

9Children

Preconcy is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10Medical disclaimer

Preconcy is an informational tracking tool. Its predictions and insights are estimates based on the data you enter and general patterns. They are not medical advice and must not be used as a method of contraception or as a substitute for professional diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your health.
